    The new loader

    The two clovers keep turning the whole time (outer clockwise, inner counter-clockwise). The inner clover's 4 petals fill as the site loads. When ready, the clovers spin once more and a circle opens onto Home.

    Motion

    Everything moves on one rhythm (128 BPM). Motion happens around the content — text settles quickly and stays still while you read. Switch Motion off in the bar at the top to see calm mode.

    • Scroll both waysCards rise in from below or above; artworks pan slowly like a camera; tiny clovers drift faster than the content.
    • Speed leanScroll fast and the cards lean 1–2°, then settle — try it on this card.
    • RibbonThe KOTOHA ribbon speeds up with your scroll and reverses when you scroll up.
    • SparklesOn a computer, a few clover sparkles follow the mouse. Not on phones.
    • Clover progressThe clover in the top bar fills as you go down the page and empties going up.
    • Calm modeWith motion off (or the device's "reduce motion" setting) only short fades remain — the loader clovers still turn, slower.
