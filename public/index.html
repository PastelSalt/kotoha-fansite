      About Kotoha

      Kotoha is a singer and VTuber with AiceClass. She's the vocalist of the duo HaKoniwalily together with Hanon, and a support vocalist for HoneyWorks.

      She debuted as a VTuber on July 8, 2023, and streams talk, singing and games every Tuesday and Friday around 21:00 JST. Her fans are called Kotorisu, and her fan mark is 🍀 (not 🥦!).
