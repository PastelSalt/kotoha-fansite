## 📋 Overview
**Kotoha** (ことは) is a Japanese female singer and VTuber. She is the vocalist for the duo unit **HaKoniwalily** (ハコニワリリィ) alongside Hanon, and also serves as a support vocalist for the famous creator unit **HoneyWorks**. She is affiliated with the VTuber agency **AiceClass** (あいすくらす), which was established by HoneyWorks.

Kotoha debuted as a VTuber on **July 8, 2023**. Her fanbase is called **"Kotorisu"** (ことリス), and her image color is **green**.
