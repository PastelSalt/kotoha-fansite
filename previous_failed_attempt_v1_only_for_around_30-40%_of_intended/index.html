About Kotoha

Kotoha is a bright, playful vocalist and VTuber affiliated with AiceClass and HoneyWorks. Vocalist of duo unit HaKoniwalily alongside Hanon, she touches millions of listeners with her gentle harmonies and covers.

She's also an avid gamer (VALORANT Diamond rank!) who welcomes everyone with warm streams every Tuesday and Friday at 21:00 JST.
